## Scope and cautions

- None of these repositories or pull requests duplicates the seven existing case studies listed in `case-studies/README.md`.
- The shortlist spans a major technology vendor, a foundation-led project, a commercial design-system project, and community-maintained projects. These are practical context labels, not a formal governance audit.
- **Search outcome for the strict rejection requirement:** none of the ten screened candidates is confirmed as a maintainer rejecting an accessibility proposal on its merits. Web Chat #5923 is the closest match: a changes-requested review was followed by the author's own test and withdrawal. Its author, not a maintainer, closed it. This is a documented near-match, not a qualifying rejection. WordPress #13671 was accepted through its SVN workflow, despite the mirror PR being closed by a bot. The other screened candidates were merged or had sparse review evidence. The search did not find a confirmed merits-based rejection.
- A candidate's PR description is evidence of what its author claimed, not independent proof that a fix works. The case-study author should inspect the diff, linked issue and every human review before scoring.
- AI-generated summaries or bot reviews should not be counted as human reviewer engagement.
